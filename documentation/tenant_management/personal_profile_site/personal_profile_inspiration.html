Writer / portfolio patterns (2025–2026)

Home: Name, tagline, professional photo, primary CTA (“Read my work”, “Download CV”).

Writings: 3–6 featured pieces — mix of on-site articles and external newspaper/magazine links with publication name.

About: Long bio, location, languages, social links.

Achievements: Timeline of awards, speaking, education.

Community: Boards, clubs, affiliations with logos.

Downloads: PDF papers, CV, chapters.

Contact: Email + optional form; social strip.
