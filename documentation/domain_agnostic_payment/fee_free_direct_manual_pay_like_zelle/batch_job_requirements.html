Batch Job Requirements: Fee-Free Manual Payments

This document outlines batch job requirements in event-site-manager-batch-jobs to support manual payment reporting and reminders.

1. Reminder Job

  • Nightly job to notify organizers about open manual requests.
  • Group by event, include requester count and total amount.
  • No auto-void or expiry enforcement (open-ended requests).

2. Reconciliation Report

  • Weekly report per event with requested vs received totals.
  • Breakdown by manual method (Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, Cash, Check, Other).
  • Export to CSV for finance teams.

3. Audit & Monitoring

  • Log failed email or report generation runs.
  • Surface batch job status in admin analytics dashboard.
